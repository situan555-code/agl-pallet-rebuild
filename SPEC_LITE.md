### 4.3 · `/how-we-work`

```
H1: A Structured, Reliable Supply Process
BODY: In high-volume manufacturing environments, consistency is everything. Our logistics and sourcing process is designed to remove uncertainty from pallet procurement by combining structured planning, vetted supplier networks, and coordinated transportation management.
LIST:
Understand Requirements
We begin with a detailed review of your operational needs, including production volume, pallet specifications, load requirements, and delivery cadence. This initial assessment allows us to identify potential constraints, forecast demand patterns, and determine the most efficient sourcing strategy.
Beyond specifications, we evaluate handling environments, storage methods, and transportation variables to ensure the recommended solution performs reliably under real-world conditions.
Source Through Proven Manufacturers
Once requirements are defined, we align your needs with trusted manufacturing partners selected for their capacity, quality controls, and consistency.
Our supplier network is built around reliability and scalability. We prioritize manufacturers capable of meeting recurring volume demands while maintaining structural standards and predictable lead times. This ensures your supply chain remains stable even during seasonal shifts or growth phases.
Coordinate Logistics
AGL manages third-party transportation to ensure on-time delivery. We coordinate routing, scheduling, and load planning to minimize delays and optimize freight efficiency.
By overseeing logistics directly, we create a single point of accountability between manufacturing and delivery. This reduces communication gaps, improves scheduling accuracy, and helps prevent production interruptions caused by material shortages.
Ongoing Adjustment
Manufacturing demand is rarely static. As production volumes shift or operational needs evolve, we adjust sourcing strategies, supplier allocation, and transportation schedules accordingly.
Our ongoing management model allows us to respond quickly to increases in demand, specification changes, or distribution adjustments. Instead of treating supply as a one-time transaction, we manage it as a long-term operational partnership focused on stability and performance.

### 4.5 · `/products`

```
H1: Pallet & Material Handling Solutions
BODY: Designed to match your operational needs — not generic assumptions.
LIST:
Stock Pallets
Reliable, high-volume supply sourced through trusted manufacturers to meet recurring demand without unnecessary customization or delay.
Our stock pallets are designed for businesses that value consistency, speed, and cost efficiency. With standardized dimensions and proven load performance, they’re ideal for warehousing, retail distribution, logistics, and export operations. Every pallet is produced under strict quality controls to ensure durability, structural integrity, and dependable performance across multiple handling cycles.
Engineered Pallet Solutions
Using Pallet Design System (PDS) methodology, AGL designs pallets precisely tailored to your load requirements, weight capacities, and operational conditions — without overbuilding or overspending.
Every supply chain has unique demands. From dynamic loads in transit to static warehouse storage and racking systems, engineered pallets ensure optimal performance under real-world conditions. Through detailed analysis of product weight, stacking patterns, handling methods, and environmental factors, we develop pallet specifications that maximize strength while minimizing unnecessary material usage.
Crates & Dunnage
Custom and stock crate solutions designed for secure transport and specialized shipping needs.
When products require added protection beyond standard palletization, our crates and dunnage systems provide the structural support and containment necessary for safe transit. Whether shipping heavy equipment, sensitive components, or high-value goods, we deliver solutions engineered to minimize movement, absorb impact, and withstand demanding logistics conditions.
Shipping Blocks
Supplemental material handling components designed to enhance stability and support palletized and specialized loads.
Shipping blocks play a critical role in reinforcing load integrity during storage, handling, and transport. Whether used to elevate products, create spacing for forklift access, stabilize irregular items, or distribute weight more evenly, these components improve safety and reduce the risk of damage throughout the supply chain.
