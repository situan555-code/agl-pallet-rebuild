WHAT AGL PALLET DOES
A Single Point of Contact for Pallet Supply
AGL Pallet is a pallet brokerage serving manufacturers across food and beverage, pharmaceutical, building materials, and industrial sectors. We work directly with trusted pallet manufacturers to source and deliver truckload quantities of pallets, managing third-party logistics and coordination so customers don’t have to.
By acting as a single, accountable point of contact, AGL ensures pallet supply aligns with production schedules, volume needs, and operational realities—reducing complexity and keeping material moving without disruption.

WHY AGL EXISTS
Built From Inside the Manufacturing World
AGL Pallet was built from firsthand experience on the manufacturing side, including navigating capacity constraints, material shortages, and pricing volatility during high-pressure periods like COVID. That background shapes how we operate today—knowing when to push for efficiency, when to push back to protect production, and how to design fit-for-purpose pallet solutions without creating unnecessary complexity.
The result is a more transparent, realistic approach that protects timelines, supplier relationships, and manufacturing continuity.
WHO AGL SERVES
Serving High-Volume, High-Expectation Operations
AGL Pallet serves manufacturers and distribution-focused businesses across building materials, pharmaceuticals, plastics, chemicals, and food and beverage—organizations where pallet reliability directly impacts production and shipping.
We work with owners, COOs, purchasing teams, and warehouse leaders at both regional and multinational operations that require truckload quantities on a recurring basis. If consistent pallet supply is critical to keeping your operation running, AGL is built to support you.
